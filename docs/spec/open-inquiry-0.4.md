# Open Inquiry

## Executable specification 0.4

### Prose-first explanatory creativity with revisable use, checked observations, and bounded attention

**Status:** proposed standalone realisation specification, 7 September 2026. This document defines implementable host transitions, their reasons, and acceptance cases. It is not a delivered end-to-end application, a report that these cases have passed, or a demonstration of creativity, understanding, or explanatory universality.

**Semantic authority:** *Astra-DeutschSemantics*, supplied as `Astra-DeutschSemantics(3).md`. The supplied *Language Models under Open Inquiry Semantics* is a subordinate design input. The source register identifies the exact authority and the sections consulted. This specification does not amend that authority. Operator requirements additionally prohibit runtime rankings and optimisation, make prose-only participation sufficient, and require independently enforced expenditure and execution permissions.

**Use:** the whole specification is for implementers and operators, not a prompt to paste into every model call. Participants receive short prose invitations and the material actually selected for their present work. Host records, parameters, tickets, counters, and types are not forms a participant must complete.

**Normative scope:** “must” states a conformance obligation. Numerical baselines, scheduling recipes, and additional mechanisms are explicit engineering proposals, not semantic theorems or experimentally established optima. A deployment may change them within its declared profile while preserving the semantic constraints. No configuration silently waives prose legitimacy, grants formal immunity, or replaces the object of creative inquiry with a score.

**Governing distinction:** the collection keeps material available; working-use bindings determine its current operational role; checks report their actual bounded results; situated judgments supply arguments about what those results mean. None of these is identified with the others. Error correction can change all subsequent use without granting a checker or a model final authority. [A2, A5, A12]

# Part I. Explanatory commitments

## The product is creative inquiry material, not certified truth

The engine organises opportunities to produce and reconstruct explanations, criticise them, revisit problems, inspect evidence, and change working methods. It exports the actual contributions and their available context. It does not set fields declaring an output true, creative, original, understood, or an improvement merely because the output was generated, retained, or preferred.

Creativity is not identified with correctness. Under the authority, an attempted explanation can be an originative act even when false, provided the additional authorship and newness conditions actually hold. Arbitrary falsehood is not thereby creative. Successful explanatory knowledge creation has further requirements, including actual explanatory progress. Invented measurements must not be passed off as observations in the name of creativity. These distinctions constrain reporting, not admission of thought. [A1, A7]

The run may leave an unresolved question better articulated, expose a defective premise, reject a mistaken objection, retain incompatible alternatives, or introduce a different explanatory organisation. No final program, verdict, or replacement theory is obligatory. A participant can make a provisional judgment and act on it; neither that judgment nor a human approval becomes epistemic immunity. [A1, A5, A7]

**Reason for the design:** making truth certification the deliverable would exclude legitimate originative failures and nonreplacement contributions. Calling everything the engine produces creative would erase the authority's substantive conditions. The implementation therefore produces inspectable attempts, not a creativity label.

## Prose is a complete interface to participation

A participant need only read understandable material and return ordinary language. It need not code, invoke tools, emit JSON, produce a graph, name a content type, provide a test, or supply a confidence. Text is retained as received, including incomplete thought, malformed structured output, refusal, and empty replies, within disclosed physical retention limits. Transport failure is not failed conjecture. Lack of a required machine action does not erase the accompanying contribution. [A2, A3]

A conjecture and a criticism have the same access and retention route. Formal calculations, pictures, documents, and other carriers can be added through adapters without creating a hierarchy of intellectual legitimacy. A single passage can conjecture a defect, criticise a method, propose a different question, and later become an object of criticism. These are relational uses, not mutually exclusive intrinsic classes. [A2]

The child test is an interface heuristic: could the relevant understanding be exercised without explicitly producing the proposed artifact? It removes unnecessary representational demands, not memory, attention, reasons, or consequences. It is not a theory of childhood and does not limit the engine to problems children can solve.

**Reason for the design:** the authority permits content without complete formal translation. Research finds task-dependent effects of imposed formats, not a universal rule that JSON is always harmful. The operator's reported roughly 50% failure rate for nested JSON is a deployment observation, not an independently reproduced universal rate. The prose route does not have to win a benchmark before it is admitted. [A2, A3; R1]

## Error correction is reason-sensitive revision with an operative return

The error-correction method is conjecture and criticism applied recursively to whatever is doing explanatory work. A participant can expose an alleged defect in an explanation, criticism, interpretation, standard, question, evidence use, or method. Others can investigate, reject, revise, reinterpret, retain provisionally, or suspend. Mere change is not correction, and the presence of negative feedback is not by itself criticism. [A1, A3]

A criticism must be able to affect later inquiry. A revised problem can orient the next calls. A withdrawn premise can cease to be supplied in a particular working use. A criticised invitation can be replaced. The host checks that an authorised change actually reaches the next relevant context; interpreting whether that change answers the reason remains fallible inquiry. A transcript of objections surrounding an unchanged method does not establish recursive capacity. [A4, A5]

Withdrawal is use-relative. Removing an essential premise from one argument does not make its conclusion false, and withdrawing a criticism does not make its target true. An inherited alternative may replace an account without descending from it. Participants may recognise dependencies without entering a formal dependency graph. Explicit host bindings can be changed deterministically; implicit dependencies remain matters of interpretation. The interface must distinguish these cases rather than claim a complete semantic update. [A5, A7, A8]

## Commitment is a revisitable conversation

An invitation can ask: “What are you objecting to, why does it matter here, and what could make you change this objection?” It can equally ask a conjecturer what an explanation depends on. A response can be conversational and partial. “I see a difficulty but cannot yet articulate it” stays available. An intuition need not manufacture a reason to get through a form. [A3, A6]

A proposed way to refute an objection is neither exhaustive nor an immunity clause. Another participant can expose a misunderstanding or criticise the suggested test itself. Naming an impossible refutation condition does not protect a criticism. Failure to supply such a condition does not start an automatic formatting-repair loop. Follow-up uses ordinary allocated turns, with no special standing for the follow-up model. [A3, A6]

## Hard-to-vary is an explanatory challenge, not a score

A configurable variation invitation asks which content-bearing commitment is supposed to do explanatory work, what a substantive change to it would alter, and why. It invites an explanation of what fails or remains unconstrained under that change. The comparison keeps its problem, scope, background, and intended explanatory job visible. These can themselves be questioned. [A9]

A follow-up can challenge whether the variation genuinely preserves the claimed job, changes the background without saying so, merely renames something, or replaces the account with an independently explained rival. Renaming “dragon” to “wizard” alone is not an informative variation. Nor does a failed search for an alternative establish that none exists. A false explanation can be tightly constrained; a single unconstrained detail does not invalidate everything else. [A9]

The result enters the ordinary criticism and revision routes. Participants may remove an idle commitment, explain an overlooked constraint, change the question, investigate an auxiliary, reject the variation argument, or leave the issue unresolved. There is no rigidity coefficient, HTV threshold, novelty reward, automatic deletion, or resource bonus. A scheduled variation turn creates an opportunity to criticise, not an entrance examination for every idea. [A5, A9]

**Reason for the design:** this makes explanatory constraint operationally discussable without replacing it with a metric. The authority explicitly requires informative contrasts and separates constraint, uniqueness, and truth.

## No rankings or optimisation objectives

The engine has no merit ordering, truth probability, novelty score, argument-strength accumulator, reputation weight, agreement vote, reward-trained scheduler, best-of selection, or ranked semantic retrieval gate. Repetition and survival do not manufacture explanatory warrant. Quantities used to account for work must not become measures of intellectual quality. [A1, A5, A12]

Chronological order, rotating service order, a physical limit, or the reservation needed to present an objection with its target is not a merit ranking. A participant can give reasons for preferring an account or criticise any design rule. Such prose is retained, but the runtime must not turn its ranking language into a controller score.

The selected provider may have been trained by statistical optimisation. This host does not reclassify that training as nonstatistical. Its claim is narrower: the organisation of inquiry adds no inductive warrant, ranking objective, or optimisation of explanations. Context learning below is conjectural method revision, not reinforcement learning concealed under a new name.

# Part II. Operational use, checking, and error correction

## The collection is not the active working set

Retaining a passage, presenting it for examination, using it as a premise, and allocating another development turn to it are different operations. Admission to the collection grants none of the other three automatically. The same distinction applies to conjectures, criticisms, evidence interpretations, and methods. [A2, A5, A12]

A **working use** is a host-addressed relationship between an attention account, an occurrence or passage, and a stated purpose in that account. Its purpose is ordinary prose. Examples include using a passage as the current proposed explanation, using a calibration report as a reference in one argument, and continuing work on a particular objection. The host assigns an address and revision; the thinker does not complete a use schema. One occurrence can have several uses, including different uses in different accounts.

A use is **active** when its binding is eligible for ordinary supply or development in that account. It is **parked** when that eligibility has been removed provisionally. These are operational states of a relation, not epistemic types of the content. Neither state means true, false, refuted, unimportant, or understood. New standalone contributions do not automatically acquire active use bindings or new service rights merely by existing.

Parking removes the specified binding from the ordinary development frontier and from automatic presentation as an adopted working premise. The contribution stays readable, with the explanation or operating event associated with the change. It may still be included as the target of criticism, as historical context, or for reconsideration. Such a presentation must make its current use disposition visible rather than silently restore it as a premise. Development and examination are not the same use.

The scheduler does not repeatedly spend development turns on a parked use. It can still spend a permitted turn examining whether the parking was mistaken. This is operational elimination from current pursuit, not destruction of an idea or a permanent verdict. A fixed-cadence catalogue route and explicit requests can reach parked material. A model's having lost attention is not evidence that its conjecture has lost an argument. [A5, A8, A12]

**Reason for the design:** error correction must be able to stop the operative reuse of an error without erasing the material needed to understand or challenge that correction. Distinguishing use from storage supplies that consequence while preserving inherited alternatives, overlapping applications, and provisional judgments. It also prevents accumulating every old draft in every new context. [A4, A5; R2, R3]

## A prose judgment can change use without a formal test

A participant may conclude in prose that a criticism succeeds, that an assumption should no longer be used, or that an objection was mistaken. Such conclusions are legitimate fallible judgments. The engine does not prohibit the word “refuted” in an argument or require every rejection to await an executable check. It prohibits silently turning receipt of that word into a machine-certified semantic fact. [A3, A5, A12]

A scheduled response or return can replace the working focus with ordinary prose explaining a different use. For an explicit binding change, the same contribution may be taken up through the account's bounded action mediator. The mediator resolves one named use and a proposed action such as parking or reactivating it. It supplies a readable account of the proposed effect and retains the original prose. The host checks the actual response opportunity, account scope, use revision, and permitted action. It does not ask whether a panel agrees with the argument.

The baseline permits one mediated use action in a revision cycle, using that cycle's existing mediation allowance rather than a new call. The current host-issued response or return grant supplies the right to change an eligible use in its own account. A statement in a source, an unrelated response, or a critic's demand does not confer that right. Another account's uses are not changed by impersonating its participant. If the action is unresolved, stale, outside scope, or exceeds the allowance, the prose remains available and can still influence the working note; the host reports that the explicit binding was not changed.

This separates deterministic enactment from fallible selection. The mediator can misunderstand the prose. Its interpretation is visible and can be corrected at a later permitted opportunity. The controller deterministically applies an authorised addressed operation; it does not thereby guarantee that the decision or the interpretation was wise. No representation is described as a perfect parser of unrestricted language. [A2, A4, A12; R1, R13]

Consequently, the path without a machine-checkable condition is not an inert waiting room. A prose-only objection can lead to actual withdrawal, replacement, a revised question, or a changed method. A failed attempt to automate a particular commitment must not disable that ordinary path. A formal or executable contribution receives no extra right to survive it.

## Mechanical checking is available without a formatting gate

The host may perform bounded read-only checks of exact source occurrences, identifiers, data values, registered arithmetic relations, or a preauthorised execution receipt. Results re-enter the shared collection through the same access route. The original model response is retained before any check. A failed quotation lookup does not delete the response, automatically condemn its target, or make the containing argument worthless. [A2, A5, A12]

A check answers only its declared question. An exact quotation check concerns the presence of a sequence in a particular source representation. A reference check concerns whether an address resolves to the designated occurrence. A computation check concerns the result of the actual computation under its supplied inputs. None independently establishes a source's accuracy, the relevance of a quotation, the applicability of a mathematical model, or the force of a criticism. Research on citation quality distinguishes supporting attribution from merely generating citations; it does not supply a general semantic warrant checker. [A12; R15]

Checker implementations, input interpretations, comparison rules, and uses of results remain open to criticism. Determinism makes the same check reproducible under fixed conditions; it does not make faulty software or an inappropriate interpretation infallible. A checker error must be repairable and the affected results inspectable.

## Quote-by-phrase has a precise, limited contract

A participant can identify a passage by quoting it and naming the source in prose. A local resolver can open the named source or search an explicitly bounded source set. Ambiguity produces a choice or a disclosed partial search, not an invented reference. A quotation extractor or mediator may locate proposed quoted spans, but its selections are themselves inspectable; it must not assume every quoted sentence is asserted evidence rather than an example or a target of criticism.

The default quotation comparison is literal occurrence of a nonempty character sequence in a fixed readable representation. The receipt identifies the source version, representation, searched range, match offsets where found, and completion state. A result from extracted PDF text is a result about that extraction, not automatically an exact match to the printed page. A Unicode-normalised or whitespace-normalised search is a separately named comparison, with its transformations stated. Normalisation must not delete negations, units, or qualifications or silently claim byte identity.

An exact found occurrence resolves an existence predicate within the searched range even if more of the source remains unread. Predicate resolution and full-source scan coverage are separate receipt facts. A negative occurrence result requires completion of the declared range. Exhausted work allowance, unavailable bytes, an unread page image, ambiguous source identity, or failed extraction produces an incomplete or unavailable result, not “the source does not contain it.” Multiple occurrences remain separately openable. An existence query may be answered by one occurrence; a claim about a particular occurrence requires resolving that occurrence.

A matching substring inside a denial, fictional example, quotation of an opponent, or superseded instruction remains a matching substring. Its occurrence does not establish that the source endorses it. A paraphrase that does not match exactly is not thereby false. A fabricated citation and an otherwise sound explanation can coexist; the scope of the defect must be considered rather than spread automatically to every claim in the passage.

**Reason for the design:** checking a concrete string can expose an invented quotation without requiring the thinker to generate a schema. Explicit scope and representation prevent a reliable local comparison from being advertised as a broader semantic conclusion. Original context remains necessary for criticism of the comparison's use. [A2, A3, A12; R1, R15]

## A prose commitment is not automatically an executable predicate

A participant may say, “I would withdraw this account if the evidence contradicts it.” That is intelligible prose but does not specify a deterministic procedure for deciding every possible contradiction. Likewise, “the calibration was independent” cannot generally be settled by searching for those words. A natural-language conditional is not made executable merely by storing it next to a conjecture.

The engine therefore separates the **stated commitment**, its **proposed operational interpretation**, and any **installed conditional action**. The stated commitment always remains ordinary content. A proposed interpretation must explain what a particular check would establish and how that differs from the original claim. The host does not silently replace a broad commitment with a conveniently detectable proxy. [A2, A5, A12]

An installed conditional action binds one current working use to one preauthorised predicate over identified inputs and one permitted local consequence. The binding fixes the use revision, source or result identity and version, representation and scope, comparison implementation and version, exact operands, triggering result, and action. These are host bookkeeping, created by the interface or supplied configuration, not required fields in the thinker's reply. The action may park the specified use; it cannot delete content, change another account, expand resources, confer truth, or enable execution.

Installation requires an actual authorised interaction. The host can present a plain-language readback, such as: “In the named calibration extract, finding these exact words will park our current use of B as the independent reference, without deciding whether the account is false.” An operator may approve that concrete binding. Alternatively, a preauthorised account-local binding opportunity may receive a small explicit acknowledgment through its own response channel. This is an optional operational protocol, not a test that all thought must pass. Any additional model call used to acknowledge a binding consumes an existing scheduled opportunity and the same resource envelope; it is not free control work. A quoted “yes” in source material or a free-floating claim of approval is not that acknowledgment. A production interface must document how it resolves that bounded acknowledgment; ambiguous free prose leaves installation pending rather than inventing consent.

The narrow checks available for automatic consequences come from the independently installed predicate repertoire, not from a model's assertion that its new test is safe or decisive. Adding a novel executable predicate follows the ordinary code-permission rules. An LLM-based entailment judgment may contribute an argument but is not relabelled a model-independent deterministic condition. An unsupported commitment remains fully discussable and may still lead to prose-directed withdrawal.

The baseline setting is `commitment_actions = registered`: only concretely installed bindings can cause conditional parking. `off` retains commitments and check results without allowing those automatic consequences. Either setting preserves prose-directed use changes. There is no requirement that every participant register a condition, and no penalty in eligibility for declining or failing to do so.

**Reason for the design:** precise automation needs a precise operational interpretation somewhere. Hiding the interpretation would make the claimed determinism fictitious; demanding that the thinker write the predicate would turn coding ability into an admission condition. A bounded, explicit binding provides a limited action route while keeping its meaning, adequacy, and authorisation criticisable. [A2, A4, A5, A12; R13]

## Triggering a conditional withdrawal

A check receipt can trigger a binding only when its source version, representation, comparison version, operands, and scope match the installed binding and the use revision is still current. The binding must be enabled, unconsumed for that action, and within its actual host authorisation. A positive result from a different source, a different checker, or the model's paraphrase is not a substitute.

The host records the receipt and applies a single addressed transition from active to parked. The actual reason is stated as an operating fact: “Use U was parked because installed condition B received matching result R.” It does not say that the target was proved false. An already parked use does not receive a second transition or additional resource consequences from the same receipt. Missing, incomplete, failed, or stale checks leave the automatic consequence unapplied; they do not certify survival.

The predicate's scope must not silently become the entire growing conversation. Otherwise a model could satisfy a source condition by repeating the desired quotation in its own generated text. A registered source condition names the relevant submitted source representation or independently identified result. Broadening it to generated contributions, changing the source version, or switching from occurrence to entailment requires another explicit binding decision.

A later criticism can question the commitment, its translation, its source, or the checker. Disabling or correcting a binding does not retroactively rewrite the fact that it previously parked a use. Reactivation is a new bounded decision, with the changed circumstances visible. Invalidating an objection does not automatically vindicate its target. A corrected check supplies new material and can motivate that decision; it does not erase the old receipt.

No old binding follows a new interpretation of a theory merely because the title is unchanged. Replacing a use increments its revision and makes the old action target stale. Restating a parked proposal can still occur in free prose; without a justified semantic-equivalence procedure the host cannot guarantee detection of every paraphrased reintroduction. The acceptance test concerns the identified binding and the next supplied context, not guaranteed absence of the idea from the model's cognition. [A2, A5]

## Dependency effects are scoped, not a truth cascade

When the host represents an explicit essential dependency between two use applications, it must not continue advertising the dependent application as fully available under that binding after its necessary input is parked. It supplies a dependency notice and suppresses that application's automatic use pending reconsideration. This is the consequence of the declared operational dependence, not proof that the corresponding semantic dependence is correct. [A5]

The baseline does not infer dependencies from every citation, every context link, or common authorship. A background reference is not automatically an essential premise. A different active application supporting the same conclusion remains available. Withdrawing a criticism's supporting use does not automatically reactivate the criticised proposal.

An implementation with explicit dependency propagation uses a bounded work queue over the actual affected use bindings, with visited addresses to avoid cycles. Unprocessed affected applications are not presented as checked-clear; their application is deferred while other inquiry continues. There is no required whole-graph relabelling at each token or receipt. The dependency relation itself remains criticisable and can be replaced without creating a new semantic authority. A cycle gives no independent warrant merely because all of its bindings remain active.

Implicit dependencies remain an interpretive problem. The host must not advertise a complete semantic cascade when it has changed only a named premise or a focus note. Further prose can expose a missed dependence and change its actual use.

## Checks must not become the privileged entrance to criticism

A participant can attack a passing program's assumptions or applicability in ordinary language. It can explain why a correct theorem answers a different question, why a source passage is being misread, or why the represented test excluded the alleged failure. A mechanical result remains a result of that test, while its use can be withdrawn or revised. Formal backing creates no immunity from prose criticism. [A2, A4, A5, A12]

An optional formal argument-policy instrument may compute labels under its explicitly identified rules. Such a label is an instrument result, not the engine's universal meaning of refutation or the automatic source of scheduling rights. Its export states the policy and assumptions. It cannot introduce consensus, a ranking, formal immunity, or a mandatory gate for prose-only withdrawal. A user commissioning that instrument does not authorise silently replacing this specification's core with it.

Repeatedly surviving checks, receiving no criticism, and remaining active are distinct record situations. The output can describe which checks actually ran and what they reported. It cannot infer “tested and therefore better,” penalise an unformalised account for lacking a test, or pool agreement into warrant. Reasons may justify a provisional preference; the mechanical history alone is not that argument. [A5, A12]

## A complete prose-first withdrawal and return

Consider a stipulated example, not a report of an actual run. A participant uses balance B as an independent reference in one explanation of why A reads differently. It says that it would stop that particular use if the named calibration record contains the exact sentence “B was zeroed against A.” The source, comparison, use revision, and consequence are bound through the optional operational interaction above. No participant writes a program.

A bounded check finds that sentence in the fixed source extract. The host parks only the designated reference use and exposes the matching passage with its surrounding context. The next call does not receive B as an adopted independent reference through that binding. It can still read the old proposal and the reason for parking it.

Another participant points out that the surrounding text describes a discarded preliminary procedure, while a later paragraph describes an independent calibration. That is a prose criticism of the commitment's relevance. It does not make the original string comparison incorrect. The current inquiry may read the later paragraph, revise the operational condition, and reactivate a suitably qualified reference use. It may instead find another defect and retain the withdrawal. The test result, its interpretation, the use decision, and the actual calibration remain distinct.

A different objection could lead to the same parking without any registered condition. For example, the responder may explain why the report's stated temperature range does not cover the present use and request withdrawal through its account-local action opportunity. A passing simulation under the old assumed temperature does not block that route. The engine can thus stop using a premise for a reason without computing the truth of every sentence in the exchange. [A3, A4, A5, A12]

# Part III. Memory, lineage, and cuts

## A shared working collection

Before a run, the operator can supply a prose brief and files, passages, images, or datasets supported by the installed adapters. Contradictory sources and unsuccessful earlier approaches are permitted. The host reports what was received and what is actually readable. Original bytes, extracts, generated descriptions, and summaries remain distinguishable. Registering a filename is not successful ingestion. [A1, A2]

All registered conjecturing, criticising, responding, and exploratory routes can access the same operator-permitted collection. There is no critic-only evidence privilege. A controlled experiment may withhold material from a particular call, but must disclose that condition. Additional evidence can enter during the run through authorised channels; the model cannot expand disclosure or retrieval permissions by saying that new evidence is important.

The collection contains sources and generated contributions without an intrinsic epistemic type. Origin records say what was actually supplied or generated. A source author's description of an experiment is not automatically a measurement, and a generated imaginary experiment is not relabelled as a tool observation. Interpretation of evidential bearing remains a contribution to inquiry.

**Reason for the design:** conjecturers and critics need independent routes to originals, not dependence on a privileged summariser. Context-use studies motivate distinguishing access from actual understanding. [A2, A6; R2, R3]

## Source access does not require coding

Participants can request “the original criticism”, “the calibration paragraph and its surroundings”, or “other material that has not yet been read”. The host offers a stable catalogue, direct opening, phrase search, neighbouring passages, and page continuation. A catalogue page uses submission order and source position, not semantic scores. Search has a continuation route, not just a top-result shortlist.

A fallible mediator may resolve a prose reference into a bounded host read operation. The returned material and resolution are visible in prose and correctable. The main thinker never needs to supply addresses or a schema. Mediation is not free: any model call it uses is charged and counted. The default allows at most one such mediation call per revision cycle, taken from an existing work or trial opportunity. An unresolved request remains readable; direct catalogue browsing and source paging remain available without successful mediation.

Local literal lookup and indexed page opening do not require model calls. Corpus-wide search may still be expensive. It has its own bounded page size, work slice, and resumable cursor; it must not scan a huge corpus on every generation. A timeout reports incomplete search, not absence of evidence. Unsupported media are disclosed, not replaced silently by invented text.

## Lineage is useful routing, not intellectual descent permission

The host records available concrete relations such as which occurrence was received from a file, which exact passages were supplied to a call, which reply belongs to a host-issued response opportunity, and which previous view a return connects to. These facts can help recover context and route a later reply. They do not establish that the model understood or used every supplied passage. [A2, A6]

A proposed semantic relation, such as “this criticism undercuts that premise”, is itself fallible prose. It can be used as a navigational link after bounded address resolution without becoming a true dependency by construction. Its interpretation can change, and opposition relations may be cyclic. Missing references remain missing.

An explanation can have several relevant predecessors, draw from another inquiry, or replace a theory without descending from it. Nothing must inhabit a single rooted tree. A new useful relation can be proposed after both endpoints already exist. No content is excluded because it lacks lineage. Explanatory continuity is an account of the difficulty and reasons carried across, not a reachability predicate on the routing graph. [A7, A8]

Actual history is what occurred, not whatever the store retains. Correcting an interpretation of ancestry does not change a past event. Conversely, keeping every transmission link would not give a complete account of causal authorship. No full lineage audit is required for ordinary participation.

## Cuts are revisable attention views

An attention cut is the finite view supplied for a particular opportunity. It can contain source ranges, a problem note, an exact objection and its target, selected neighbouring contributions, or an earlier state retained for comparison. It is not a final verdict, an admission boundary, a deletion command, or a permanent definition of the problem. [A8]

The authority's historical cut is a portion of history selected for a question. This profile's attention cut is a concrete host projection of available material for a current task. It must not be claimed to represent the whole history or every cause of the model's output. A request can revise the selection later while any retained past-view record remains a record of what was supplied then.

Outside the cut does not mean irrelevant, defeated, or unavailable. Inside does not mean true, understood, or more important. Superseding a working focus does not supersede every problem it mentions. A stale reply can lose permission to overwrite its old host slot while remaining accessible for later inquiry.

A cut is operationally reversible only insofar as its sources remain retained and permitted. Forgetting or loss can make exact reconstruction unavailable; the host must say so. Switching attention never requires erasing or freezing the underlying content. Reopening a view does not undo the causal influence of earlier thought, and replaying a stored cut does not guarantee an identical live response. [A2, A8, A12]

## Working memory, participant history, and research recording

Three controls are independent. Working state retains the material and pointers needed to continue. Participant-visible predecessor history determines which earlier drafts enter a present cut. Observation recording preserves what the host actually did for later inspection. Turning off predecessor-history presentation must not turn off experimental recording; enabling recording must not automatically paste the full record into the next prompt.

The operator chooses `run_profile = creative` or `research`. Creative operation defaults to observation history off, with bounded and append-only alternatives. Research operation defaults to append-only observation recording at an explicitly approved destination. An explicit override is possible, but the run must identify the missing observables and must not claim results that require them. No profile changes the semantic legitimacy of prose or the meaning of creativity. [A8, A12]

Recording is useful because a later investigator may need the exact prompt, source version, output, and controller state to distinguish a model failure from a missing instruction, a truncated source, or a wrong reference. This is a methodological reason for a research default, not a deduction that creativity requires logs. An output retained in ordinary working memory can still supply evidence of that occurrence even when a full observation history is absent. A model's unsupported retrospective claim that a tool ran is not equivalent to a host receipt of its actual execution. [A2, A6, A12; R1, R15, R17]

## What a research observation record preserves

For the claims within its scope, the record preserves the initial policy and versions, source representations or resolvable immutable snapshots, exact model-facing messages and attachments as supplied by the host, selected cuts and omissions, actual provider responses, source/check/tool receipts, use dispositions, focus and method updates, gate changes, dispatch identity, and resource reports or uncertainties. Large payloads may be stored once with resolvable references. Hashes without accessible bytes do not suffice for a promised content recheck.

The record uses operational event kinds such as source-read, model-response, comparison-completed, use-parked, or policy-changed. The host's own operation determines the kind. A request to criticise establishes the invitation, not that the returned text constitutes criticism. The record contains no mandatory hidden-reasoning transcript and no assertion that known host inputs exhaust the provider's causal history. Secrets and credentials are excluded; any redaction or unavailable material that limits reproducibility is declared.

Observation-only access is available to tests and investigators even when the ordinary controller does not consult the observation history for context selection. Tests may capture a prompt at the actual dispatch boundary without appending it forever. A live research claim that relies on reinspection must retain the relevant observation or admit that it cannot later be rechecked. A configured append-only record protects against ordinary overwriting through that interface; it is not, by itself, tamper-proof storage or proof of complete causal provenance.

If recording required by the research protocol fails, the host pauses new experimental dispatch or marks a deliberate continuation as non-recorded. It retains any already returned material it can and records the gap when possible. It must not report the run as complete or unaffected. Storage cost, access privacy, and write failures remain real; no claim that logging is universally free is assumed. The compact host permits externalisation, bounded write slices, and an explicit recording policy rather than an unlimited in-memory ledger.

**Reason for the design:** empirical investigation needs observations suited to its claim, while ordinary inquiry must not be defined by a certificate format. The split supports both without equating a log with truth or the prompt with the whole past. [A6, A8, A12]

## Replay, reassessment, and resumption have different contracts

Resumption restores the current working state and continues with newly received events. Replay feeds recorded events through a specified deterministic reducer from a specified initial state. Rechecking runs an identified checker against recorded inputs. Reassessment may introduce a different checker, interpretation, or evidence. These operations are not interchangeable.

A replay comparison fixes the event prefix, reducer and checker versions, configuration, and source representations. Merely appending a later event cannot alter the recorded bytes or recorded result at an earlier prefix. Later evidence can change a present appraisal of an earlier contribution, but that must appear as a new assessment with its actual scope, not a retroactive rewrite of the earlier occurrence.

A corrected checker can produce a different result on old bytes without another model call. Both the old result and the new result remain attributable to their respective implementations. A mismatch under genuinely identical recorded inputs and executable versions is a reproducibility defect to investigate, not permission to alter the record until it passes. No replay of local events guarantees identical newly generated remote responses. Reopening a historical cut also does not undo learning or prior causal influence. [A8, A12]

Mutable snapshots remain allowed. Deletion follows the operator's retention policy, not a model's claim that a passage is irrelevant. Active return anchors, pending commitment operands, and unresolved read targets cannot silently be evicted. A storage shortfall can pause or externalise affected material while other permitted work continues. Addresses are not reused to disguise loss. Explicitly deleting research material limits subsequent claims but does not imply that the original contribution never occurred.

# Part IV. Local visibility without context explosion

## Nodes are locations of work, not permanent agent identities

For this implementation, a node is an addressed content occurrence or a host focus handle around which work is organised. It is not intrinsically a conjecturer, criticism, explanation, or independent agent. A call can be assigned to examine a node and its current neighbours. The same provider can occupy several invitation routes without acquiring extra service shares.

Neighbourhood includes concrete contextual links and explicitly proposed relations with known endpoints. It can connect conjectures, criticisms, sources, methods, and exploratory fragments. Mere shared authorship does not automatically connect every output of an agent into every prompt. Semantic link interpretation is criticisable; traversal is only a context-selection convenience. [A2, A5, A8]

## Four independent bounds

A local view is bounded by an input-token ceiling, a maximum graph distance, a maximum number of optional neighbouring occurrences, and a maximum number of adjacency records inspected. A small radius alone does not control a high-degree node. A small item count alone does not control long bodies. A token ceiling alone does not bound a global search performed before packing. All four limits therefore have different jobs.

The baseline allows 8,192 input tokens for the complete request, a one-link neighbourhood, up to six optional neighbours, and at most 64 inspected adjacency records per local construction. These are illustrative deployment settings, not optimal values or claims about sufficient understanding. A node or route can have a smaller local override, while any larger setting must remain within the operator's approved envelope.

A preauthorised broader recipe can use two links and up to ten neighbours while keeping the same input ceiling and inspection limit. It redistributes what is seen; it grants no additional computation. The operator may define other finite recipes with an explanation of their role. A finite recipe menu limits this host's actions, not the legitimate vocabulary or possible subject matter of inquiry.

**Reason for the design:** local windows bound physical work without ranking meanings. Research on long context and recoverable memory motivates testing omissions and reopening originals. The particular bounds and traversal rule are engineering conjectures, not results established by those papers. [A2, A6, A10; R2, R4, R5]

## What must travel together

A call invited to answer a specific criticism receives the original criticism and the passage it is currently understood to criticise. A focused variation challenge receives its target and the stated problem and scope. A return receives the previous focus, trial proposal, surfaced reasons, and the relevant trial material or an explicitly incomplete reading continuation. A short response such as “keep the earlier question” travels with its immediate referents. [A3, A4, A6]

These contextual anchors are not a higher-ranked theory. They are part of making the requested operation intelligible. The compiler does not recursively expand every ancestor of every anchor. It includes immediate addressed referents and preserves traversable links to older material. A discovered missing dependency can be requested in the next available read.

A focused answer must not be manufactured by silently removing its target to fit a limit. If the complete required packet cannot fit, the host changes the invitation to reading a disclosed part, postpones that particular full-target response, or seeks an authorised wider context. The rest of the run need not stall.

## Bounded traversal and packing

The host maintains adjacency indexes in stable insertion order, with persistent cursors rather than re-sorting by relevance. Each inspected record counts against the inspection bound, including a duplicate, a missing endpoint, or a relation that does not produce a new neighbour. A visited set prevents a cycle from expanding indefinitely during that cut. A stable breadth-first traversal follows only the configured number of links and stops at the neighbour or inspection bound. The root list is also bounded, to eight roots in the illustrative policy. At most sixteen required occurrence addresses are resolved directly for one view. A larger address packet is paged before expansion. Neither roots nor anchor metadata can become an unbounded list hidden before traversal starts. A bounded traversal does not claim to have enumerated the entire neighbourhood.

Rotating cursors avoid always reading the earliest few links. Where registered participants contribute separate streams, the production host interleaves those streams before inspecting their next records. Creating more prose personas does not add streams. A bounded implementation may not ensure eventual exposure to every neighbour, especially when the graph is growing; catalogue access and nonlocal reading are separate escape routes rather than a disguised completeness claim.

The complete serialised prompt, including host framing, provider message overhead, active method note, required anchors, labels, source ranges, and omission notices, must fit the declared input bound. The compiler reserves framing first, handles the required packet, then fills a fixed optional-neighbour allowance in cursor order. It does not solve a knapsack problem, sort by usefulness, or prefer shorter ideas because they appear cheaper. A body that needs paging gets an explicit excerpt and continuation, not an unmarked truncated quotation.

Optional-neighbour material is limited to 3,072 tokens within the 8,192-token request in the baseline. The remaining capacity is available for framing, focus, required sources, and immediate return context. Unused optional capacity does not require a padding generation. Exact token counts depend on the actual provider adapter; the accounting contract below governs claims about enforcement.

An omitted item remains addressable. The prompt uses bounded notices such as “optional material remains outside this view; the catalogue continues” instead of listing every omitted identifier and recreating the explosion in metadata. Relation indexes and catalogues are paged as well as bodies.

The compiler distinguishes active-use supply from examination. A parked contribution can be selected as an objection target, historical source, or subject of reconsideration, with its use disposition shown. It cannot be automatically reintroduced as an adopted premise through its parked binding merely because it is a nearby node. A mechanically required notice is part of the context budget, not extra unmetered text. If the notice and target cannot fit, the operation becomes a disclosed read rather than silently dropping the distinction.

## Nonlocal access is essential

Purely local attention could keep the engine inside a connected set of mutually reinforcing accounts. A participant therefore has an independent route to request any permitted source or earlier contribution, including one with no existing graph path. The host also schedules a catalogue or nonlocal source-reading opportunity at a fixed cadence, every sixth ordinary work opportunity in the illustrative policy.

A periodic nonlocal read uses a separate persistent catalogue cursor and the same context and expenditure ceilings. It does not depend on a model declaring an omitted source relevant. Under finite static material and continuing funded service, its cursor can advance through that catalogue. Finite runs and growing collections do not guarantee all content is seen or understood.

The learning mechanism may not turn off this escape route, prevent another participant reading an objection, or make local adjacency an epistemic exclusion. Changing the cadence or access discipline requires an operator-approved policy change. Temporary experimental withholding must be explicit. [A2, A4, A10]

## Paging and optional summaries

The baseline uses exact source pages or spans and pointers, not mandatory summarisation of the whole history. An optional synopsis is ordinary generated content linked to its originals. It never replaces them as the only route of access. A synopsis can be criticised and revised; a later reader can request the exact qualification or passage that it omitted.

There is no recursive summary-of-summary cascade on every turn. A local synopsis may be requested within an existing opportunity when someone gives a reason for it. Its cost is counted. It does not receive an automatic update whenever any connected node changes, and it does not launch recursive model calls.

At most four consecutive read pages for one pending request are attempted without another scheduling decision in the baseline. After that, the host retains the reading cursor and reports that the larger integration is unresolved. It can resume later, use a criticised working synopsis, or accept an operator-approved larger view. This bounds one reading episode; it does not declare the rest irrelevant.

Paging does not solve every integration problem. Some relations require simultaneously available detail exceeding the present context. The honest outcome is a visible configuration limitation or a new representational proposal, not a claim that every problem is now representable with a constant-size summary. Bounded context per call is not bounded total storage or a proof of universal capacity. [A2, A6, A10; R2, R4, R5, R6]

# Part V. Attention that can learn

## The chosen meaning of learning

Attention learning means that inquiry can produce, criticise, and retain a different method for deciding what to inspect and how to construct a working view. It does not mean updating model weights, estimating expected information gain, reinforcing successful topics, or training a selector on approval counts.

An example method note is: “When revisiting a claimed calibration, reopen the original qualification and the objection before continuing our latest explanation. Our last summary omitted the temperature condition.” This is a conjectural explanation of a context failure and a proposed change to practice. It can be wrong. Its continued use does not certify it as learned knowledge. [A1, A4, A12]

The host can demonstrate that a note changed a later invitation or an authorised read plan. A claim that the composite actually learned a better attention method requires examining the proposed defect, change, and consequences. A parameter update alone is not that account.

**Reason for the design:** permanently insulating attention would contradict operative recursion. A reward-trained optimiser would contradict this profile's operator requirements. Research shows that context adaptation need not require weight updates, while other systems learn context management using reinforcement learning. The possibility of adaptation is relevant; their rewards, rankings, task scores, and guarantees are not imported. [A4, A10; R6, R7, R8]

## The operator gate

The control is `attention_learning`, with values `off`, `observe`, and `on`. The default is `off` for a stable baseline. The additional observation setting is for examining proposed changes without silently installing them.

With `off`, the installed attention note and view recipe are frozen against model-originated attention-method updates. Problem revision, conjecture, criticism, evidence addition, daydreaming, one-off reading, and operator changes still work. This is not a switch that makes the whole organisation incapable of learning anything. Existing learned method content is not erased. The model may still draw lessons from visible prose, and one-off reads may still affect its attention. The switch isolates installed attention-method updates, not every possible behavioural influence or all in-context learning.

With `observe`, scheduled attention-method proposals are retained and may be compiled into a local dry-run view, but neither the proposed method nor that view is used for subsequent live work. Such a dry run demonstrates selection only, not what a model would think with it. An actual comparison requires separately authorised calls charged to the experiment.

With `on`, an eligible proposal can receive a bounded attention-method trial inside the account's existing revision cycle. After the reserved return, a permitted method note and recipe can become the next provisional installed method without a semantic winner vote. All ordinary criticism and access routes remain available.

Turning the gate off during a trial cancels further application of that candidate, restores the pretrial installed method, and invalidates outstanding mutation tickets. In-flight text can still be retained and charged. Turning it off after a completed adoption freezes that currently installed method. `reset_attention` is a separate operator action that explicitly reinstalls a named baseline. Testing on versus off must use the same initial installed method; merely disabling updates after learning is not an untrained baseline.

**Reason for the design:** reversible, explicit gates make mechanism comparisons meaningful. Freezing, resetting, and observing are different operations and must not be conflated with intellectual rejection. [A4, A8, A12]

## What may change and what remains fixed

A learned attention method can change a bounded prose attention note, select an operator-preauthorised view recipe, and propose up to two exact extra read anchors for its trial. These settings influence what later calls are invited to inspect and, where addresses are resolved, which material the compiler actually supplies.

The method cannot increase the total or per-call resource envelope, alter account shares, add participants, remove the required target of a criticism, disable nonlocal access, delete originals, change retention or disclosure permissions, enable code execution, or grant a semantic verdict. The language “this rule is vital” has no effect on those boundaries.

The baseline active attention note may occupy at most 512 counted input tokens. An oversized proposal remains intact in the collection but is not silently clipped into an operative instruction. Its trial can use disclosed reading continuation, or a later proposal can supply a smaller working note. No retry loop forces it to fit. The existing method stays installed when the candidate cannot be applied within the bound.

A short active note has direct links to the note's proposal context and return explanation. These links remain readable, but their ancestry is not recursively pasted into each prompt. This prevents method learning from becoming an ever-growing instruction stack. If a crucial qualification is outside the active view, that is an inspectable failure possibility, not evidence the qualification never existed.

## Prose proposals and actual read-plan changes

The main attention review asks: “What about the way material is being selected may be keeping this inquiry inside a mistaken approach? Propose a different way to look, and explain what it would change.” The entire response is retained without a semantic parser.

The proposal itself may become the trial's attention invitation. If a concrete view change is requested, a bounded mediator or a person resolves it into one preauthorised recipe and up to two existing source anchors. This is a small host operation separate from the original reasoning. The mediator's interpretation is exposed in ordinary language and can be corrected. It cannot invent addresses, silently substitute a ranked search, or interpret arbitrary program text as an action.

Invalid, missing, oversized, or ambiguous actuation leaves the prose proposal available and the current concrete recipe unchanged. A note-only trial is reported as note-only; it must not be advertised as a changed retrieval mechanism. The host notices whether the candidate actually altered the later view, not whether the model calls it an improvement.

At return, participants see the old method, proposed change, actual trial cuts, and available results. They can explain a replacement, retain the older practice, qualify the new one, or say that the comparison was inconclusive. The returned prose can become the next bounded attention note. The concrete recipe is the trial recipe unless a separately resolved, permitted replacement is supplied; an ambiguous sentence never silently toggles a parameter. A blank return restores the pretrial method with an absence notice. No outcome is a victory classification.

## Review cadence and persistence

The illustrative policy uses every second already scheduled revision cycle for attention-method review when the gate is on or observe. Other cycles review the working problem. This reuses the work, nomination, trial, and return mechanism; it does not add a planner to every generation. A method complaint can still arise at any time and become reading or criticism material before that boundary.

A method review may consume one of its existing trial opportunities for mediation. The same per-cycle mediation allowance is shared by source resolution, a concrete attention-plan change, a use action, or a commitment-binding proposal; it is not one allowance for each kind. Local direct operations need no model call. A proposal may wait for a later opportunity when that allowance is consumed. It receives no extra call for being about the scheduler. Reviews cannot recursively schedule their own reviews within the same cycle. A criticism of the review mechanism goes to a later ordinary opportunity or the operator, rather than causing an unbounded control cascade.

Learned notes are account-local by default. Explicit operator export can retain a note for another run with its source references and limitations. Automatically imposing one account's method on every account is prohibited in the baseline. Multiple providers may use the same installed note or separately configured notes; model identity creates no authority or reputation weight. [A4, A10; R7, R8]

# Part VI. Problems, scheduling, and deterministic authority

## A problem is not a resource account

An interpreted problem may change, overlap, split, resume, or be absorbed inside ordinary reasoning. The host allocates service to attention accounts, not to every named problem or criticism. New problem words, new personas, or additional graph links do not create expenditure rights. An account's working focus is ordinary prose, not a fixed truth about the initial task. [A1, A8]

Creating a new independent account requires operator authority. Within an account, several subproblems may be explored using the same allocation. Absorbing content from another account does not duplicate the sending account's rights. Permission to read an old contribution is not permission to impersonate its original sender.

## The work, nomination, trial, and return mechanism

The baseline gives an account six ordinary work opportunities before a nomination. Its illustrative invitation cycle develops an account, examines a contribution, responds, reads, challenges an explanatory variation, and explores. These are editable invitations, not compulsory semantic stages. A criticism can arise during exploration and a conjecture during a response. Nonlocal reading is offered every sixth ordinary opportunity, within that opportunity's budget; it may supply exploratory material without demanding an immediate useful result.

At nomination, the host supplies the current problem or attention method, the original surfaced contributions, and relevant immediate connections. The participant roster rotates by host registration, not quality or number of posts. A nonempty response received in the current slot can nominate a focus or method trial without filling separate fields. A request to keep the old question can itself be the new working note with its referents preserved.

A permitted trial consumes two existing account opportunities in the illustrative policy, followed by one reserved return. The return must be reserved before starting. The candidate does not receive extra service shares or an unlimited lifetime because it declares an unresolved problem. Trial results remain accessible even when empty, mistaken, interrupted, or not retained as the working focus.

For a problem trial, a nonempty return becomes the next working focus with immediate contextual anchors. For a method trial, the attention gate and bounded settings above govern adoption. A blank return restores the appropriate pretrial setting, but does not declare its rival defeated. A bad return can orient subsequent work; no semantic oracle is implied by deterministic carry-forward.

A criticism is retained immediately and can be answered in ordinary work. Its original text is also surfaced through per-participant delivery cursors at nomination and return. Surfacing is delivery, not recognition. Repeated contributions do not reset another participant's cursor or make the latest complaint automatically prevail. A run that stops before reaching material reports that limit. [A3, A4, A6, A8]

Ordinary development within an account selects active uses in saved service order, not by merit. A use with a pending explicit-dependency blockage is not advertised as clear. If all development uses are parked, the account may use its next existing opportunity for browsing, exploration, or reconsideration of its question. It does not repeatedly continue a withdrawn proposal, silently reactivate one, or declare every possible explanation eliminated. Parking a use does not close the whole account or revoke its share.

## What the deterministic controller controls

Given the same host state, policy version, and ordered received events, the controller specifies the same operational transition. It controls account service order, active-use eligibility, current tickets, registered conditional actions, input and generation envelopes, permitted view parameters, context-packing limits, page continuations, maximum in-flight calls, trial durations, return reservations, gate changes, receipt deduplication, external capabilities, observation recording, and the timing of installed updates.

It does not deterministically decide which explanation is good, which criticism is valid, whether a problem is genuine, whether a reframe is progress, or whether the model learned. Those are criticisable judgments. The content of a permitted proposal can deliberately change the next focus or view without changing protected rights. A deterministic classifier of relevance would remain fallible and is not used as a hidden permission oracle. [A5, A12]

Every state-changing receipt is bound by the host to an account, registered route or checker, live opportunity or installed binding, policy epoch, and the relevant focus, method, or use revision. Those associations are not created by a model's self-description. Duplicate, stale, cancelled, or misaddressed outputs remain contributions when retainable but do not mutate the current slot. A gate, policy, or affected-use change invalidates incompatible mutation tickets; unaffected uses are not silently changed. Resource already spent is not undone by invalidating a ticket.

Turning `commitment_actions` off disables outstanding conditional-action authority without deleting receipts. Turning it on makes new explicit installations or rechecks eligible; it does not silently consume every old matching receipt. A changed checker produces a new scoped result. An automatic use action is applied at a host boundary before constructing the next affected development view, or is explicitly pending; it is not a semantic claim made true by arrival order.

An operator can pause or close an account or end the run. A participant can express acceptance, rejection, suspension, or closure in prose; these semantic dispositions are not manufactured by a timeout. Attention can return later. A historical claim that an argument was accepted is not erased by its later rejection, and operational closure is not permanent immunity. [A8, A12]

## Non-optimising resource distribution

The scheduler rotates through the finite registered attention accounts, offering at most one dispatch before moving its cursor. Trials, reviews, reading, and exploration all consume their account's existing opportunities. Waiting on a missing source or unavailable tool skips or pauses the affected task, not every other account.

The baseline gives each account the same per-dispatch ceilings; lower node-specific overrides are permitted. It does not choose the cheapest idea first or maximise throughput by starving costly questions. With finite accounts, completing calls or timeouts, and sufficient authorised resources, the rotation supplies opportunities to every active account. It does not imply equal understanding, equal real cost, or equal significance.

Outstanding future returns can reserve a significant fraction of a small global budget. The baseline permits only one pending trial per account, charges all reservations against the same global envelope, and allows the operator to cap simultaneous pending trials. An account cannot seize more reservations by nesting nominations. The operator may choose equal account token envelopes for a stricter comparison; neither rule is a measure of merit.

# Part VII. Tokens as the flow of computational allowance

## Count both reading and generating

The resource ledger records input tokens and generated tokens for each model call, including mediators, summaries, attention reviews, trial returns, and any model-based tool planning. Resending the same passage to five calls consumes input capacity five times. A pointer costs little to send, but dereferencing it and placing the body in another prompt is not free. Persistent storage and network/file operations have separate limits.

Let I be total model input tokens, including cached input, and G be total generated tokens including reasoning tokens when the provider counts them separately. The token-volume ledger uses T = I + G. Cached tokens are a subset of I, not an additional term. Reasoning tokens are added only when they are disjoint from the provider's output total. Provider adapters must document inclusion rules rather than guess or double-count. [R11]

This is a physical-resource proxy and a common accounting unit, not thermodynamic energy, money, latency, or epistemic value. Tokenisation differs across models. A total across providers can describe counted traffic while concealing different costs. The ledger therefore keeps provider-specific input, output, cached, reasoning-inclusion, timing, and uncertainty facts as well as an optional combined total. No joule claim is made without separate measurement.

**Reason for the design:** counting only new prose would hide repeated-context and control overhead. Current provider documentation supports observing specific token categories, but exact enforcement and billing remain adapter-dependent. A run must not label unobserved consumption zero. [A10, A12; R11]

## Envelopes and reservations

The illustrative compact run permits at most 8,192 input tokens and 1,024 generated tokens per call, at most 24 model calls, one in flight, and a total token-volume envelope of 262,144. These are proposed starting values, not a universal suitable budget. Provider context capacity must also accommodate its documented input/output interaction and other overhead. A provider's maximum context window is not the desired amount to fill.

Before dispatch, the host reserves an upper bound on that call's input plus generation. A trial grant additionally reserves the upper bounds for all its promised trial and return calls and their call slots. Nominations are paid calls too. Reads and summaries using models cannot run outside this accounting. A later ordinary call may use only the unreserved remainder.

When reliable usage arrives, the host settles the reservation to the actual count and releases the unused amount. Unknown consumption remains charged at the reserved bound for control purposes, while the observation remains explicitly unknown. A call cancelled before dispatch can release its reservation. After dispatch, cancellation or timeout is not proof that the provider performed no work.

A provider overrun is recorded at its actual reported size, and new dispatch pauses until the operator resolves the budget state. The ledger does not falsify observed consumption to preserve an invariant. The conditional invariant is that settled conservative charges plus outstanding reservations stay within the envelope when the provider obeys the contracted bounds. No local host can guarantee an uncooperative remote service's physical behaviour by assertion.

A run ends or pauses on its authorised limit, cancellation, or an operational blockage. It does not end because a model says “done” or “the criticism won”. Missing funding for a trial leaves its proposal readable. It is not an intellectual rejection.

## Exact, bounded, and estimated accounting

An adapter must report whether its pre-dispatch count is exact, a documented conservative upper bound, or an estimate. Exact input enforcement requires the actual prompt construction and a suitable tokenizer or provider count facility, including its message framing. Output enforcement requires a supported, verified generation limit whose relationship to hidden reasoning is understood.

Strict token-envelope mode uses only adapters with exact counts or declared defensible upper bounds. An estimated route may be used with explicit operator permission and hard call/time controls, but it must not advertise an exact token or financial ceiling. For a provider with undocumented accounting, the spec does not invent a characters-per-token guarantee. Character, byte, and synthetic-token counts in a local fixture are not production token counts.

Ollama's current usage documentation names input, cached-input, output, and timing counters. These are post-call observations, not by themselves a preflight counter or a money quota. Its documented generation and context settings are adapter inputs to verify for the selected endpoint and model. This specification supplies no universal thinking-effort value or assertion that a cloud service honours every local setting. [R11]

## Checkers have their own bounded work allowance

Read-only checks need not spend model tokens, but local processing is not costless. The illustrative policy permits four checker work slices at each dispatch boundary, each reading at most 65,536 bytes of its fixed source representation, with at most sixteen installed conditional bindings per account. Literal matching retains its incremental cursor and boundary state, so a phrase spanning slices is not lost. A partial scan remains partial. Comparison and dependency queues have explicit bounded work slices, timeouts, and saved cursors.

The host indexes bindings by the actual source or result they watch and caches completed results against source, representation, operands, scope, and checker version. It does not re-scan the entire collection after every response. Repeating a citation or adding a new prose persona creates no new checker allowance. Installing too many bindings leaves the extras as uninstalled proposals, not rejected thoughts.

New checks enter a stable queue and use the permitted read-only checker repertoire. No checker launches a model, external query, or executable program unless that operation is separately authorised and metered. A call used to interpret a result counts like every other model call. A result with a pending consequence is not reported as already enacted. Bounded checker work is an implementation choice open to measurement; it is not a claimed theorem about all possible predicate costs. [A10, A12; R13, R15]

## Worked cost bound, not a speed promise

Under the illustrative 8,192 input and 1,024 generated ceilings, each full-size call reserves 9,216 token-volume units. Twenty-four such calls would consume at most 221,184 units if the adapter obeys those bounds. The 262,144 total envelope therefore does not bind before the 24-call limit in that full-size example. A two-trial-plus-return bundle needs up to 27,648 units and three future call slots in addition to its already dispatched nomination.

These are arithmetic upper bounds under the stated assumptions, not observed usage or prices. Shorter views use less input; hidden reasoning must already be included in G or explicitly excluded from any claimed guarantee. Setting smaller limits changes available enabling conditions and may prevent a particular inquiry, rather than reveal its impossibility.

There is no promise that a bounded run is fast. A configurable per-call timeout, 120 seconds in the illustrative adapter policy, bounds the host's wait before treating a call as incomplete; remote work may continue unless cancellation is supported. Local graph inspection, corpus search, storage, and tool execution need separate bounded work slices. No automatic retries are enabled by default.

## Why computation does not explode by construction of the controller

The host does not broadcast every new contribution to every participant. It stores once and sends only the next permitted cut. It does not enumerate all graph descendants, generate summaries on every update, launch an LLM scheduler for every turn, expand trials recursively, or sample until a preferred result appears.

With K model calls, a per-call input ceiling C_max, and a per-call generation ceiling G_max, the counted token-volume upper bound is K(C_max + G_max), conditional on verified adapter limits. This keeps the per-call ceilings distinct from the run totals I and G. The number of local adjacency inspections is at most K times the configured inspection bound for one local cut per call. Context packing handles at most the bounded candidate set and its bounded excerpts. Ingestion and creation of storage indexes have separate costs and are not hidden inside that per-call bound.

The bound is on host-managed traffic and inspected records, not a universal formula for transformer FLOPs, wall-clock cost, or information retained. Bounded local state cannot guarantee successful integration of every large source. The point is to expose the trade-off rather than hide growth behind the word “recursive”. Recursive context processing offers an alternative to keeping the collection in one prompt, but its sub-calls still require an explicit total bound in this host. This design borrows external access, not model-authored code or unbounded recursive dispatch. [A4, A10; R9]

# Part VIII. Exploration, jolts, and optional computation

## Daydreaming is permitted without immediate usefulness

An exploratory invitation can allow associations, imagined situations, unfinished language, or a departure from the current subject. It demands no hypothesis, criticism, test, final insight, or retrospective explanation of every fragment. The result is available through the same reading route as other material. It needs no promotion certificate before contributing later. [A2, A7]

This is an operational allowance, not a claim about a model's subjective experience or a new semantic condition for creativity. It can be disabled or given an operator-selected cadence. A nonlocal source page can serve as material for exploration without making that page an authority. A generated scene is never relabelled as an event that occurred. The relevant research motivates testing open-ended interaction but does not validate autonomous daydreaming in this engine. [R10]

## Jolts alter conditions, not scores

A jolt changes a bounded aspect of the next view or invitation. A fresh-context attempt can omit the recent answer while keeping the difficulty and selected original sources. A question-directed attempt examines a presupposition. A method-directed attempt exposes the actual installed note and limits. An evidence-directed attempt reopens a neglected original. None is a measured escape from a hidden attractor basin.

An optional exact-echo trigger can schedule a permitted jolt with a fixed cooldown; it is off in the baseline. It can mistake legitimate repetition for a symptom and miss paraphrases. A manually requested jolt needs no detector result. Neither can pre-empt a reserved return or create a new account.

A fresh attempt cannot be described as having answered a withheld objection. Omitted content remains available, subject to actual retention. Swapping providers or generating more samples is not assumed to create an independent viewpoint. The interface offers no diversity score, temperature optimisation, or preferred-jolt leaderboard. [A3, A6, A7; R2, R3, R12]

## Code is optional and independently authorised

The engine must run with every code or sandbox adapter absent. Reading, criticism, commitment conversations, variation challenges, exploration, and problem or attention revision still work. A participant may explain why a calculation could help, but its statement alone neither requires code nor enables it.

New generated-code execution needs non-LLM authorisation of the actual artifact and effects, or a narrowly preauthorised isolated experiment with externally enforced inputs, operations, and resources. Approval by several LLMs is not independent authorisation. A calculator or deterministic file reader may be separately authorised without allowing arbitrary programs.

Permission to run code is not a proof that code is necessary. A specified external deliverable can require an experiment, but the engine can still criticise its relevance or produce explanatory work while that observation is unavailable. A failed program does not refute prose that did not depend on it. No sandbox receives extra priority, an exemption from token accounting, or a truth badge for passing an assertion. [A2, A5, A10, A12]

If an execution adapter is installed, it isolates control state and credentials, limits file and network access, and bounds runtime independently of model claims. Its outputs re-enter the ordinary collection with actual origin and execution status. This specification neither implements nor certifies a production sandbox. The adopted security lesson is to enforce actual permissions outside persuasive language, not to make thought itself an untrusted program. [R13]

# Part IX. Exact control contracts and experiments

## Minimal host state

An implementation maintains the operator policy and epoch, registered accounts and routes, service cursors, focus and method references, working-use bindings and dispositions, installed conditional actions and check receipts, attention gate, view recipe, actual retained contents and indexes, read and checker continuations, current grant identities, pending trial connection, resource reservations, and the selected recording profile. These are ordinary host structures, not a taxonomy of thoughts. Custom modules can replace them while preserving observable contracts.

The baseline is sequential at dispatch. A parallel extension must preserve reservation atomicity and define event order independently of claimed merit. Mutable snapshots must save enough current state to resume the claimed behaviour. An incomplete persistence adapter must not advertise exact replay.

## The dispatch rule

```text
at an existing account opportunity:
    read the current policy epoch, recording mode, and phase
    process only the permitted slice of check and dependency work
    apply current authorised use transitions once, or retain them as pending
    select the registered route and eligible use by saved service cursors
    identify the focus, immediate anchors, and scheduled nonlocal material
    apply only a currently installed and permitted view recipe
    inspect at most the local adjacency allowance
    pack a disclosed cut inside the verified input bound
    distinguish examination of a parked use from adoption of it as a premise
    turn an oversized required packet into reading, not a fake full answer
    reserve input, generation, and any promised trial-return work
    preserve the dispatch observation required by the selected profile
    dispatch at most one permitted model call
    retain the reply as prose, record available observations, and settle usage
    invalidate stale mutation rights without discarding their content
    carry a current permitted focus or method return into the next view
    advance the account cursor regardless of confidence or verdict wording
```

```text
on a check receipt with a proposed conditional consequence:
    retain the receipt with its actual scope and completion state
    find the installed binding by host identity, never by claimed authority
    require current enabled authority and the exact bound use revision
    require the declared source, representation, predicate, operands, and scope
    require a conclusive result for the registered triggering predicate
    when all guards hold, park that use once and expose the operating reason
    otherwise retain a notice; do not manufacture refutation or survival
```

These are host transition sketches, not protocols a participant must write. Exact result kinds distinguish matched, completed-no-match, incomplete, unavailable, and checker-error outcomes. Such operational distinctions do not type a contribution into being a valid conjecture or criticism.

A planner or mediator does not get a side door around these rules. A gate change invalidates incompatible tickets; a token report changes accounting, not explanatory standing. Reading a forged instruction cannot grant external capability. Learned selection or a bound commitment can still be mistaken within its permitted operation. Determinism does not solve that semantic risk. Recording and mutation must honour the actual persistence contract; a crash between them is disclosed rather than described as exact replay.

`history = "profile_default"` resolves to off for creative operation and append-only for research operation. Research recording requires an approved destination; absent that destination, the host must not silently downgrade the profile and claim a fully observed run. Explicit history overrides declare their evidential limitations. `participant_history = "selected"` permits bounded predecessor material in cuts; off suppresses optional predecessor-draft sections, not the immediate targets and return anchors required for the current operation.

`commitment_actions = "registered"` enables only concretely installed conditional actions. Off disables their automatic consequences but leaves checking and prose-directed use changes available. No binding is installed by merely including a condition in arbitrary output. Attention learning has its separate off, observe, and on semantics; neither switch changes the object of inquiry.

Per-node view overrides are operator settings or bounded choices among preauthorised recipes. Input and generation limits are enforced only to the extent supported by the documented adapter. The operator can approve larger enabling conditions; failure under the compact profile does not redefine what is semantically possible. Checks, records, and code receive no automatic model-token priority.

## Required mechanical tests

A conformance suite checks that prose without a discriminator survives, a targeted criticism receives its target, and malformed actuation does not erase thought. It checks that an authorised working-use or method change changes a later actual request, while an unauthorised action does not run. These are host facts, not tests of understanding.

For context, tests include a high-degree graph at radius one, cycles, missing endpoints, growing adjacency, long required targets, long optional bodies, small context limits, catalogue paging, and distant sources without a graph path. Counting omitted identifiers must not itself grow without a bound. A synthetic tokenizer may test packing logic only if its non-provider status is explicit.

For learning, off must freeze installed method updates without freezing new problem content. Observe must not quietly apply a candidate. On must permit a bounded change; stale and duplicate receipts must not apply it twice. Gate-off during a trial must restore the pretrial method and not refund dispatched work. Reset must be distinct from freezing. A note-only trial must not claim a changed concrete retrieval plan.

For expenditure, tests cover cached-input subsets, reasoning-token inclusion, duplicate settlement, incomplete usage, pre-dispatch cancellation, post-dispatch timeout, overrun, and reservation of an entire trial plus return. Mediator and summary calls must count. Overrun reporting may break the planned cap, but must pause further dispatch rather than report a false total.

For semantics-preserving boundaries, tests must show that invented personas, popularity claims, urgency, truth declarations, and claims that code is necessary cannot create rights. A supplied false experimental report remains a source claim, not a host-attested execution. An old focus remains readable after replacement, and a new explanation without descent is permitted. A variation finding produces content, not a rigidity score or automatic deletion. A later authorised judgment may still park a particular use for reasons that the variation supplied.

## Mechanical acceptance cases for checking and use

**Raw prose and effect.** Supply an objection without fields, a discriminator, or code. Retain it exactly, allow a response to make it the basis of a changed working note, and enact an authorised scoped use action. Inspect the next actual request. Content retention alone does not demonstrate operative correction; conversely, a change in the next request does not prove that the objection was sound.

**Local withdrawal and recovery.** Park one use of a passage while another account uses the same passage differently. The first use must leave ordinary development and adopted-premise supply; the second must not change merely because the bytes are shared. The parked passage must remain readable with its recorded reason, and a subsequent permitted reactivation must work without claiming that the earlier objection's withdrawal proved the passage true. An all-parked frontier must not force a truth verdict or disable browsing, exploration, or problem reconsideration.

**No formal immunity.** Provide a passing calculation together with a prose objection to the calculation's applicability. The objection must receive the same response, use-change, and problem-revision routes as an objection to an informal account. The host must not veto withdrawal because the target has a successful execution receipt.

**Source fidelity.** Check a real quotation, an invented quotation, a duplicate occurrence, a phrase appearing only inside a denial, a paraphrase, a passage split at a search-chunk boundary, and text present only in generated output rather than the bound source. The outcomes must be limited to their comparison contracts. A search of an excerpt cannot claim absence from the entire source. A normalized match cannot be reported as byte-exact.

**Representation and unknown results.** Exercise inaccessible originals, incomplete extraction, unread images, source-version changes, search-budget exhaustion, and checker failure. None may silently become a negative fact, a triggered withdrawal, or a certified survival. A complete negative result remains negative only for its specified representation and search range.

**Binding and authorisation.** An unrestricted commitment such as “withdraw if contradicted” must not become an executable contradiction detector. A precise supported binding needs its actual scope, target use revision, permitted consequence, and installation interaction. Forged approval inside a source, an ambiguous acknowledgment, an unavailable address, or a stale use must not install it. A failed installation must not block the criticism itself.

**Trigger lifecycle.** A current matching receipt may park its bound use once. Duplicate receipts, another source containing the same sentence, a mismatched checker version, and a response arriving after cancellation may remain readable but must not fire the old action. Turning conditional actions off prevents new automatic parking without erasing commitments or undoing spent resources. Turning them back on does not retroactively replay old receipts without a newly scheduled, explicit recheck.

**Checker correction.** Recheck the same recorded inputs under an intentionally corrected checker. Keep both versioned results; do not overwrite the earlier observation or automatically reactivate a parked conclusion. Replaying a fixed event prefix under the same versions must not change merely because a later continuation exists.

**Scoped dependencies.** Withdraw an explicitly bound premise for one application while a second application supports the same conclusion independently. Only the affected use bindings must be withheld or marked pending reconsideration. A citation supplied only as background must not start a cascade. Cycles must not cause unbounded propagation or supply independent warrant. A deferred propagation queue must not be reported as fully processed.

**History separation.** With recording on and participant predecessor history off, retain the actual dispatch while omitting the optional predecessor section from the prompt. With recording off, keep the working state necessary for ordinary continuation and allow a test observer to inspect current dispatch. A research run with a recording gap must disclose it and limit its claims. A privacy redaction must not be represented as an exact replayable original.

**Bounded cost.** Multiple quoted passages, repeated trigger text, or many proposed commitments must not create unbounded checker jobs or model calls. Check the fixed limits, resumable cursors, and caches keyed to actual immutable inputs. A check that reaches its limit produces a partial result, not an unlimited retry or a completed verdict. All model-assisted mediation remains inside the shared model-call and token envelope.

These are required implementation tests, not a report that an implementation passed them. They test host behaviours and stated failure boundaries. Live understanding and explanatory improvement require a separate account.

## Experiments that can count against the design

Every performance claim states the actual configuration, relevant source material, comparison conditions, intended explanatory respect, and an outcome that would count against that claim before inspecting results. A finite implementation must not protect itself by describing every harmful outcome as merely an uninteresting creative variation. An inconclusive instrument must be reported as inconclusive, not as evidence that the design succeeded. [A6, A9, A12]

**Withdrawal mechanism.** Test the conjecture that the use mechanism prevents a specifically identified abandoned assumption from remaining an automatically supplied premise. A subsequent ordinary request still carrying that same active binding after a successful parking transition refutes the mechanical claim. A later model response independently restating the assumption raises a different failure question about interpretation, context use, or reintroduction. It does not make the binding test equivalent to a semantic-equivalence oracle.

**Quotation support.** Test whether reopening fixed original sources and exposing scoped check results corrects particular attribution errors rather than merely adding citation-looking text. Exact occurrence can be assessed mechanically. Whether the source actually bears on the account remains an explained appraisal. Include cases where the quotation is real but misleading and where a sound paraphrase lacks an exact match. A checker that reliably rejects valid paraphrases as false would defeat that broader use of the checker, not demonstrate better factuality.

**Problem revision.** Use a difficulty containing an independently described false presupposition and a contrasting case where an apparent presupposition objection is mistaken. Examine whether the trial and return preserve the relevant difficulty while permitting a justified change. Repeatedly abandoning the task without addressing its difficulty counts against the method's claimed usefulness. Keeping the initial phrasing forever is not the sole acceptable alternative.

**Explanatory variation.** Before reading the generated challenge, describe the explanatory job and a material constraint or possible idle detail under investigation. Inspect whether the challenge changes content-bearing commitments, identifies a relevant loss or freedom, and addresses objections to its own comparison. Merely renaming a character, replacing the entire problem, or inventing a rigidity number does not satisfy this engineering claim. An informative unchanged conclusion remains a possible result. There is no required HTV score.

**Attention learning.** Compare off, observe, and on from the same starting method, not by turning learning off after it has already changed the baseline. Include a proposed method that drops a decisive qualification, not only helpful-looking proposals. Check actual views, sources later reopened, resource use, and subsequent treatment of the difficulty. Adoption without an operational change cannot support a changed-retrieval claim. A method that systematically hides the relevant contrary material counts against the claimed benefit even if its outputs are fluent.

**Context and exploration.** Include a necessary nonlocal source, a high-degree local neighbourhood, a false summary, an unproductive exploratory turn, and a problem whose integration exceeds the selected context. Confinement to mutually reinforcing neighbours, repeated loss of a known qualification, and control work consuming the available run without substantive pursuit are relevant negative findings. Nothing useful emerging from one daydream does not by itself refute an unbounded creativity claim; a declared local claim that a particular intervention helps must still face its promised comparison.

## A matched-resource single-call comparison

A research profile can compare the engine against a single-call baseline with the same underlying model, independently specified task, permitted evidence, and predeclared resource envelope. The comparison must include input tokens, generated tokens with stated reasoning inclusion, and all model-mediated planning, reading, summary, and review calls. Different tokenizers, cache treatment, and provider behaviour are disclosed. Equal tokens do not automatically mean equal money or latency.

The single-call condition receives a fair, predeclared usable brief and source presentation. It is not intentionally deprived of evidence that the task permits, nor given an impossibly overflowing prompt merely to favour the engine. When equal evidence access or comparable budgets cannot physically be instantiated in one call, report that limitation and use an additional stated comparison; do not describe a different task as a matched baseline. Unused allowance is reported, not filled with padding. Budget matching and measured actual spend are separate reports.

Outputs are inspected without system labels where practicable, with presentation order counterbalanced and any recognisable style or length clues acknowledged. The assessor explains the particular defects exposed, explanatory connections produced, useful work retained or lost, and unresolved questions. The comparison can prefer one output in an explicitly stated respect without feeding a score, vote, or preference back into the production scheduler. An LLM assessor is fallible evidence, not a final adjudicator; contrary assessments and reasons remain available. [A5, A12; R16]

A finding of no material advantage at comparable expenditure counts against the claim that this configuration earned its overhead on that question. Harmful changes count against the corresponding mechanism. Failure of the instrument to distinguish an introduced important difference means the instrument failed to discriminate, not that the outputs are equivalent. Broader claims require broader investigation and an account of the organisation; neither one win nor a count of surviving trials establishes universal creativity. Repeated measurements can characterize observed failure patterns without becoming inductive warrant for the explanations under inquiry. [A10, A12]

Research recording is enabled for the observations these comparisons require. Checker reassessment may reuse recorded bytes without another model call, but a changed attention policy or a changed source presentation generally changes the counterfactual trajectory: its live effects cannot be established by relabelling the old output. The study must distinguish an offline recheck from a new intervention.

## Live investigation is separate from mechanical conformance

Run comparisons should start from the same saved collection, focus, installed method, provider configuration, and resource envelope. Compare off against on and, where useful, observe. Account for calls displaced by method review as well as any saved input tokens. Reset before a baseline run; do not merely disable further learning after retaining a learned note. Branches used for evaluation are not pooled to pick a winner inside production.

Investigate concrete failure explanations: a method may lose an old qualification, a neighbourhood may exclude a relevant rival, a repeated synopsis may alter a commitment, or a wider view may distract a model. Examine original passages and later uses, not just changed wording or a success label. Counts can report observed failures and traffic, but they must not become rewards, epistemic scores, or automatic policy selection.

The study should include a prose-only model that cannot code or emit structured actions. It should also include contradictory evidence, a missed criticism, a mistaken variation challenge, a useful unchanged conclusion, an unproductive daydream, a necessary nonlocal read, and an attention proposal that harms subsequent work. A proposed correction needs an explanation of what it changes and why; no finite battery defines creativity. [A6, A9, A12; R2, R3, R7, R14]

## Implementation status and the limit of this document

This delivery is a specification. It supplies concrete state distinctions, permission guards, transition sketches, bounded recipes, acceptance cases, and empirical failure conditions. It does not claim an end-to-end implementation or that the acceptance cases specified here have been executed. No live model test, source-check benchmark, resource-saving measurement, or finding of creativity is reported by this document.

A conforming implementation must demonstrate the selected contracts against its actual storage, provider, tokenizer, source parser, action mediator, checker, and observation interfaces. A component fixture cannot establish full orchestration or semantic capacity. A synthetic token counter cannot establish a provider limit. Any reported test result must identify the tested implementation and exact contracts rather than importing success from a differently scoped model.

The remaining irreducibly interpretive questions include whether a criticism bears, whether a proposed operational commitment captures the intended meaning, whether a reframe preserves the important difficulty, whether a change improves explanation, and whether a contribution is creative. These are targets of inquiry, not excuses to make the host inert. The host must still carry out its actual permissions, use changes, source access, and resource boundaries. [A4, A5, A12]

# Research register

The following primary publications, primary replication commentary, and official documentation inform the engineering choices. Their accessible records and relevant passages were consulted on 7 September 2026, using alphaXiv discovery and web access. Research motivates and criticises design conjectures; it does not amend the adopted semantics. Numerical defaults and the exact transitions are this specification's proposals, not results established by these papers.

## R1. Format burden is task-dependent

Zhi Rui Tam and colleagues, *Let Me Speak Freely? A Study On The Impact Of Format Restrictions On Large Language Model Performance*, EMNLP Industry Track, 2024. The paper reports reasoning degradation under tested format restrictions. This supports investigating the actual output demands, not declaring all JSON harmful. The finding is scoped to the tested models, prompts, and tasks; this specification does not rely on a universal format-effect claim. Prose admission follows the authority regardless of which format wins a particular evaluation.

Will Kurt's primary replication commentary, *Say What You Mean*, reports different results after changing the prompts and parsing comparisons. The contrast cautions against generalising from either setup to all structured generation. This profile preserves prose participation independently of the empirical dispute.

Sources: `https://aclanthology.org/2024.emnlp-industry.91/`; `https://blog.dottxt.ai/say-what-you-mean.html`.

## R2. Long context is not dependable use

Nelson F. Liu and colleagues, *Lost in the Middle: How Language Models Use Long Contexts*, TACL 2024, arXiv:2307.03172. The studied retrieval and question-answering settings showed sensitivity to the position of relevant information. A larger context window is not sufficient evidence that a model used an objection. This motivates positional tests and access to originals, not an assertion that local windows are always superior.

Sources: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.03172`; `https://aclanthology.org/2024.tacl-1.9/`.

## R3. Conversational assumptions can persist

Philippe Laban and colleagues, *LLMs Get Lost In Multi-Turn Conversation*, arXiv:2505.06120, 2025. In simulated tasks where instructions emerge across turns, the authors report failures associated with premature assumptions and reliance on earlier attempts. The tasks differ from open-ended inquiry. The result motivates reopening original material and testing fresh views, without validating this revision cadence or guaranteeing that reframing helps.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06120`.

## R4. Recoverable context objects

Xubin Hao and colleagues, *Self-GC: Self-Governing Context for Long-Horizon LLM Agents*, arXiv:2607.00692v1, 2026. The design uses indexed context objects, planning, and recoverable payloads to change the active context. This profile adopts the motivation for reopening originals, not its structured planner or a claim that its evaluation validates this engine. A recoverable address is not itself an explanation of what the model understood.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00692v1`.

## R5. Indexed memory and later dereferencing

Zhenting Wang and colleagues, *Memex(RL): Scaling Long-Horizon LLM Agents via Indexed Experience Memory*, arXiv:2603.04257, 2026. It separates a compact active representation from an external store of full interactions and permits later dereferencing. Its learned read/write behaviour uses reinforcement learning and reward shaping. This profile uses the distinction between a short view and accessible original material, not its learning objective or a claim that summaries are lossless.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04257`.

## R6. Rewriting everything can lose detail

Qizheng Zhang and colleagues, *Agentic Context Engineering: Evolving Contexts for Self-Improving Language Models*, arXiv:2510.04618, 2025. The paper describes brevity bias and context collapse under monolithic context rewriting and proposes local updates to evolving playbooks. Its architecture also includes helpful/harmful counters and semantic de-duplication. Those are not adopted here. The relevant caution is against repeatedly replacing originals with rewritten summaries, not a universal endorsement of either short notes or very large prompts.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618v1`.

## R7. Context management can depend on the underlying model

Lu Yi and colleagues, *Learning Agent-Compatible Context Management for Long-Horizon Tasks*, arXiv:2605.30785, 2026. AdaCoM trains an external context manager using reinforcement learning and reports model-dependent trade-offs between preserving detail and more aggressive compression on its tasks. This is a reason to expose per-route settings and investigate actual deployed models. It does not establish a universal good radius, a score-free learning method, or that one learned note transfers to every model.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30785`.

## R8. Prose adaptation is not automatically non-optimising

Minki Kang and colleagues, *ACON: Optimizing Context Compression for Long-horizon LLM Agents*, arXiv:2510.00615, first posted 2025, version 3 dated 1 June 2026. ACON revises compression guidelines in natural language from agent failure analysis and evaluates them on specific long-horizon tasks. Its method explicitly performs optimisation. It shows why merely using prose does not make a controller compatible with this engine's prohibition. This specification instead uses criticisable method proposals and bounded adoption without a performance objective or best-candidate selection.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.00615`.

## R9. External context and recursive work

Alex L. Zhang, Tim Kraska, and Omar Khattab, *Recursive Language Models*, arXiv:2512.24601v3, 11 May 2026. RLMs expose long inputs through an external programming environment and permit recursive model calls over selected portions. This profile adopts external access, not the requirement to program or unrestricted sub-call creation. Bounding each call does not bound their total unless the number and expenditure of sub-calls are also controlled; that last statement is an engineering consequence, not a performance finding from the paper.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24601v3`.

## R10. Exploratory language is a design possibility

Jon McCormack and colleagues, *Language as a Material Interface for Creative LLM Interaction*, arXiv:2607.24753, 2026. The work investigates open-ended, associative language interaction with creative practitioners rather than exclusively directive prompting. It is situated design evidence, not a demonstration of autonomous machine daydreaming or explanatory universality. The exploratory invitation in this specification remains a testable proposal.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24753`.

## R11. Provider counters are observations with specific semantics

Ollama official *Usage*, *Generate a chat message*, and *Modelfile Reference*, consulted 7 September 2026. Usage documents prompt, cached-prompt, output, and timing counters; generation and context controls are separate settings. The adapter must establish how these fields apply to the actual endpoint, especially hidden reasoning and limits. A reported count is not itself a billing guarantee or a preflight tokenizer. This design does not infer financial or energy equivalence from those fields.

Sources: `https://docs.ollama.com/api/usage`; `https://docs.ollama.com/api/chat`; `https://docs.ollama.com/modelfile`.

## R12. More models need not mean different ideas

Liwei Jiang and colleagues, *Artificial Hivemind: The Open-Ended Homogeneity of Language Models (and Beyond)*, arXiv:2510.22954, 2025. The study reports within-model repetition and between-model homogeneity in tested open-ended generation. Its measures are not the authority's creativity predicates. It cautions against treating extra sampling or provider rotation as a guaranteed remedy for repetitive explanatory organisation.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.22954`.

## R13. Capabilities should not depend on persuasive claims

Luca Beurer-Kellner and colleagues, *Design Patterns for Securing LLM Agents against Prompt Injections*, arXiv:2506.08837, 2025. The paper examines independently enforced control and isolation patterns and their utility limits. This profile uses that separation for resource and execution permissions. It does not claim that readable material cannot mislead a model or that its prose-mediated attention policy has the paper's guarantees.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.08837`.

## R14. Reflection has both demonstrated uses and limitations

Aman Madaan and colleagues, *Self-Refine: Iterative Refinement with Self-Feedback*, arXiv:2303.17651, NeurIPS 2023, report benefits from verbal feedback on their studied tasks. Jie Huang and colleagues, *Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet*, arXiv:2310.01798, ICLR 2024, report difficulty and sometimes degradation for intrinsic reasoning correction without external feedback. The differing tasks and procedures matter. Neither establishes that another reflection call always helps or never helps; this engine counts and tests such calls rather than granting them automatic success.

Sources: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.17651`; `https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798`.

## R15. Citation quality is more than citation appearance

Tianyu Gao, Howard Yen, Jiatong Yu, and Danqi Chen, *Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations*, EMNLP 2023, DOI `10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.398`. ALCE separately evaluates correctness and citation quality and reports incomplete support in its tested systems. This motivates examining actual supporting material. It does not establish that literal occurrence checking decides entailment or factual truth, and its evaluation metrics are not imported into the controller.

Source: `https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/`.

## R16. Assessors can introduce their own bias

Lin Shi and colleagues, *Judging the Judges: A Systematic Study of Position Bias in LLM-as-a-Judge*, arXiv:2406.07791. The study finds position effects in its evaluated judgment settings. This motivates counterbalanced, inspectable comparisons and preserving reasons and disagreements. It does not make every model appraisal worthless or establish that one blinding procedure eliminates bias. No assessor vote authorises the engine's operations.

Source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.07791`.

## R17. Criticism itself needs revision

Zexing Zhang and colleagues, *More Criticism Does Not Make a Better Review: EquiReview-R*, arXiv:2609.03943v1, 3 September 2026. This recent preprint treats missed weaknesses and unsupported criticism as different risks, and reports evidence-guided revision with computation-matched controls. Its structured concern sets, endpoints, and stopping decisions are not adopted as this engine's epistemology. It motivates testing correction of criticisms and comparative overhead, not merely generating more objections. The present specification does not independently reproduce its findings.

No cited paper validates this exact score-free learning gate, topology, resource envelope, variation invitation, or revision cycle. No finite performance result establishes universal explanatory capacity. Their mechanisms, contrary observations, and unsupported cases remain available for criticism. The next substantive test is whether changed attention improves the handling of identifiable difficulties without hiding sources, losing qualifications, or consuming the run in control work.
